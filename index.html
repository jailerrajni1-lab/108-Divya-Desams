Hari Sarvottama Vayu Jivottama · ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय · ॐ नमो नारायणाय

The 108 Divya Desams are the Vishnu temples glorified in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham of the twelve Alvars. Data compiled from traditional Divya Desam lists — histories are brief summaries; map pins are approximate town-level locations, tap “Directions” for live navigation. Your visits, notes and photos stay on this device for offline use. Sign in with Google in Settings to sync progress and uploaded photos securely when online.
